The owner was silent (their account), so this is a self-interruption. What it was, I cannot say from the
numbers alone:

- **Level.** Over 70.1-71.0 s the input was -43.2 dBFS. Echo on the line was -56 to -62 dBFS (13-19 dB
  lower) and real caller speech -12.5 to -15 dBFS (28-30 dB higher). It is a loud echo, a quiet voice,
  or a transient.
- **Voicing.** Autocorrelation peak 0.67 and spectral centroid 490 Hz, like speech (callers 0.53-0.73,
  410-595 Hz); echo stretches 0.41-0.47 and 585-1500 Hz.
- **Relation to our audio.** The envelope correlates with our reference at 450 ms over 68.9-71.1 s
  (0.54; echo stretches 0.12-0.33 at 540-660 ms), and a 150 ms dip in the bot's speech at ref 69.0 s
  shows in the input 450 ms later. The burst ends at 71.1 s, 0.45 s after the reference stops at
  70.95 s (flush). `analyze.py`'s burst detector found it (lag 447 ms, "tail"). So it is likely an
  echo burst louder than usual (the phone's AEC re-adapting, or the speaker), garbled by the ASR into
  words that are not ours. Not proven: no listening.
- AICC's recording (left channel) shows the same rise from about -67 to -48 dBFS.

**Which rule let it through: none of #9, #10 or #8 applies.**

- #9's contiguity is in the complete-utterance checks and the unheld rule. The final transcript
  "不不熟悉" shares no run of 3 units with the played text ("当液体表面的分子获得足够能量变成气体跑掉
  时..."), so its contiguity is 0.0 and it is not an echo turn.
- #10's matched hold needs a check that was all ours, 3 units or more. Check 1 had 0 units, so the hold
  was fresh and kept the old rule: `unmatched >= confirm_min_new_units` (2). "不成熟" has 2 of 3 units
  not ours.
- The correlation veto `confirm_echo_psr` (15) did not fire: PSR 6.92.

So it is the fresh-hold partial release on a garbled short echo: the case #9/#10 cannot reach, because
the garble leaves nothing of our words to match. Both guards that exist in the hold are about text that
matches ours.

**Proposed fix (not implemented).** For a fresh hold the cost of persistence is one 250 ms interval,
which the real barge-ins here did not need (113-223 ms in V1/V2, 121-155 ms here). Options, in the
order I would try:

1. For a fresh hold whose first check was empty (0 units) and whose second is short (3 units or fewer),
   require the count to persist into the next check. This delays "等一下" (check 1 already has the
   words) by 0 and a mid-word first release by 250 ms. It needs a replay on all captures first.
2. Use the level: release a fresh hold only if the near-end residual (input less the echo estimate) is
   within X dB of the line's recent caller level. This line cannot use the noise floor (it is -77
   dBFS), but this call has a 28 dB gap to the callers; V1/V2 have not been measured for it.
3. Leave it: one 1.3 s burst in 220 s at 45 dB ERL.
